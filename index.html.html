How it started

From day one to a custom-coded platform

A simple job at Pak British Institute grew into the group's whole digital setup.

Day one

Social media handler and video recorder

Hired for Pak British Institute to run social media and record videos. Website development was not part of the role.

A website in progress

A company was building the Pak British Media site

Usman first ignored it. Then he learned the work was on WordPress, and asked for it to be paused.

Why stop it

Pak British is a big institution

It will need many changes and upgrades, and may need to show an international presence. So: a fully custom-coded website.

First quote

The WordPress team quoted PKR 1.5 lakh

They agreed to code it fully custom, and the work went ahead because the contact was reference-based.

New requirement

The heads wanted the highest-end platform

The aim: no errors showing up in the future. The Laravel stack came up, and the discussion moved to it.
